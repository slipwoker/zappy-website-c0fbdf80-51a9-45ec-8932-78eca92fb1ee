Frequently Asked Questions

What types of inquiries can I send through the contact page?

You can reach out about sizing, order changes, custom requests, collaboration proposals, or general brand questions. We read every message and route it to the right team member.

How quickly can I expect a response from GRVMZ?

Our team typically replies within 24–48 hours on business days. During new collection launches or sale events, response windows may extend slightly, but we always prioritize urgent order-related messages.

Is GRVMZ open to creative collaborations or wholesale partnerships?

Absolutely. We love working with stylists, photographers, and like-minded brands. Use the contact form and select "Collaboration" as your subject — our creative team reviews these personally.

How do I stay updated on brand news and product drops?

The best way is to follow our social channels and check the journal section of the site. For early access, sign up for the newsletter — subscribers often receive first looks at limited releases.
